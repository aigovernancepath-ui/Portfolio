ILLUSTRATIVE · PRIORITIZATION

Investment prioritization framework

I structure the conversation around a consistent set of questions before recommending a delivery sequence.

  1. What strategic objective does this support?
  2. What value and evidence support the business case?
  3. What risk or deadline makes it urgent?
  4. What capacity and dependencies affect feasibility?
  5. Who approves the trade-off and records the decision?

Output: an agreed priority, accountable owner, and documented rationale.

ILLUSTRATIVE · DELIVERY GOVERNANCE

Explore the governance lifecycle

Select a stage to see its documents, responsibilities, and decision checkpoint. This is an illustrative framework; roles and requirements adapt to each organization and initiative.

01IntakeDefine the problem and intended outcome.
Documents
Opportunity submission; problem statement; initial outcome and scope.
Responsibilities
The business lead explains the need; the portfolio or PMO lead checks completeness and identifies an accountable owner.
Decision checkpoint
Is this a clear initiative with an owner and a strategic purpose? Accept for assessment, request more information, or decline.
02PrioritizeAssess value, risk, and capacity.
Documents
Business case; value and risk assessment; capacity estimate; dependency map; prioritization record.
Responsibilities
Business and technology leads validate value and feasibility; the portfolio lead presents trade-offs; the designated decision-maker agrees the sequence.
Decision checkpoint
Proceed, defer, or stop? Record the priority, capacity assumptions, and rationale.
03PlanAgree scope, milestones, and accountability.
Documents
Charter; roadmap and milestones; resource plan; RACI; RAID log; testing and change approach.
Responsibilities
The project or program lead coordinates the plan; delivery and business owners confirm responsibilities; relevant control teams review risks.
Decision checkpoint
Are scope, ownership, capacity, and delivery checkpoints agreed? Approve the plan or resolve gaps before committing.
04DeliverTrack risks, dependencies, and decisions.
Documents
Delivery status; updated RAID log; decision and change logs; milestone evidence; resource forecast.
Responsibilities
Delivery teams execute; the project lead tracks progress and dependencies; accountable owners resolve issues and escalate decisions.
Decision checkpoint
Are milestones and outcomes still achievable? Confirm the next increment, approve a change, or escalate a recovery decision.
05LaunchConfirm testing and operational readiness.
Documents
Test results; UAT sign-off; go-live checklist; support handover; training and communications; rollback plan.
Responsibilities
Business representatives accept the solution; technology and operations owners confirm readiness; the authorized owner makes the go/no-go decision.
Decision checkpoint
Is there sufficient evidence for a safe launch? Approve go-live, accept documented residual risks, or hold until gaps are resolved.
06ReviewAssess outcomes and capture lessons.
Documents
Post-implementation review; outcome or benefits assessment; lessons learned; outstanding-action log; operational handover.
Responsibilities
The business owner reviews outcomes; the project lead facilitates lessons; operations accepts ongoing ownership and tracks remaining actions.
Decision checkpoint
Close, improve, or continue monitoring? Agree follow-up owners, due dates, and the next benefits review.
